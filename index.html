The Science

Dopamine tells your brain, "do that again." Each time you scroll, anticipating the next video, your brain spikes with dopamine.

It works exactly like a slot machine. Overwhelmed, your prefrontal cortex (the logical brain) goes quiet.

Worse, your brain kills off receptors to compensate, making real life feel incredibly boring.

@BrainScience
Dopamine and the slot machine 🎰

The Fix

Friction breaks the loop. That brief gray screen acts as a "pattern interrupt."

It gives your logical brain space to ask, "Am I enjoying this, or just scrolling?"

Because swiping no longer equals an instant reward, your brain starts reviving dopamine receptors, making normal hobbies fun again.
